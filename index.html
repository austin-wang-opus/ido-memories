How this preview works

Your selected size sets the grid. Each 8 mm cell samples the colours in that part of your photo and becomes one round brick. We preserve your crop, sharpen small facial details gently, and render crisp round studs at high resolution.

Colours are simulated directly from your photo. Actual results depend on the available brick colours, lighting and image quality. Larger sizes use more bricks, so faces can retain more detail.

Grid counts are estimates based on 8 mm spacing and the listed dimensions; the final count depends on the frame allowance. Home views are styled mockups, not measurements of your room; check furniture clearance before choosing a size.
